Spotlight: Heirloom MKE

The Howell Avenue restaurant grew out of a yellow food truck, survived a fire and still serves its famous burrata

Heirloom MKE is a farm-to-table restaurant and bar at 2378 S. Howell Ave., in the storefront that was home to Lazy Susan for nine years. Nearly everything is made from scratch with ingredients from local farms and purveyors, and the menu marks which items are locally sourced and which can be made vegan or gluten-free.
