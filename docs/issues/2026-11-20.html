Spotlight: Burnhearts

A Logan Avenue tavern with a long past, an aged-beer stash and the February block party that fills the street

Burnhearts is a dimly lit corner tavern at 2599 S. Logan Ave., a few blocks east of Kinnickinnic Avenue on a mostly residential stretch of Bay View. It is a beer bar first: when Urban Milwaukee visited in 2013 it counted 24 taps and two casks, and the bar's website still keeps separate draft, bottle-and-can and cocktail lists.
