Bay View history

Sulfur at English Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 3.0
1887

Bay View governed itself as a village for eight years before its roughly 4,000 residents voted in 1887 to join Milwaukee, becoming the city's 17th Ward. The trade gave up independence for city services residents wanted, such as sewers, sidewalks and street lights. The Beulah Brinton House, pictured, was an early gathering place for the village and is now home to the Bay View Historical Society.
