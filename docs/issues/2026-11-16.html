Spotlight: Rush-Mor Records

Milwaukee's oldest record store has been spinning on the corner of KK and Potter since 1971

Long before Kinnickinnic Avenue filled up with cocktail bars and boutiques, there was a record store on the corner of KK and Potter. Rush-Mor Records, at 2635 S. Kinnickinnic Ave., opened in 1971 and bills itself as Milwaukee's oldest record store, a title local press has repeated for years. It still sells new and used vinyl, CDs, DVDs, band T-shirts…
