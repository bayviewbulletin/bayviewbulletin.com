Spotlight: McLaughlin & Hayes Hat Co.

Bay View's master hatters still block felt on century-old equipment, and won't sell you a hat online

Inside the former Boulevard Theatre at 2250 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. is something most cities lost decades ago: a working hat shop. McLaughlin & Hayes Hat Co. sells, makes, cleans and repairs hats for men and women, from fedoras and western hats to Derby showpieces and fascinators.

1872

Beulah Brinton, the mill town's teacher, librarian and neighbor

In 1872, Beulah Brinton moved into a new house on Superior Street and opened it to the rolling mill's immigrant families. Her lending library grew into Milwaukee's first branch library.
