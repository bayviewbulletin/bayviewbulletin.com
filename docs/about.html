Bay View's morning paper

The Bay View Bulletin is a free daily digest of what's happening in Milwaukee's Bay View neighborhood. It's put together every weekday morning from local news outlets, the neighborhood's own businesses and organizations, public social media posts, and city records. Every item links back to the people who did the reporting.

What we cover

Openings and closings, development and City Hall, streets and parking, schools and family, parks and the lakefront, events and live music, protests and rallies, safety, real estate, ways to help, Friday fish fries, lost and found pets, Bay View High sports, remembrances of community figures, and the ten biggest Milwaukee stories of the day.

Where we cover

Bay View as most neighbors know it: from the harbor and Jones Island south to Holt and Morgan avenues, and from I-43 east to Lake Michigan, including Humboldt Park, South Shore Park and the stretch along Kinnickinnic Avenue.

How we report

  • News from the last 24 hours, plus upcoming events.
  • Safety coverage is limited to notable incidents, and we don't name private individuals.
  • Protests and rallies are listed neutrally, for any cause.
  • Photos come from the original source or free-licensed collections, always credited and linked. If you own a photo and want it removed, tell us and it comes down.
  • Found an error? Let us know and we'll correct it.
