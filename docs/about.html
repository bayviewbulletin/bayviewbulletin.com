Bay View's morning paper

The Bay View Bulletin is a free daily guide to what's happening in Milwaukee's Bay View neighborhood, updated every morning. We gather it from local news outlets, the neighborhood's own businesses and organizations, public social media posts and city records, and every item links back to the people who did the reporting.

Where we cover: Bay View as neighbors know it, from the harbor and Jones Island south to Holt and Morgan avenues, and from I-43 east to Lake Michigan.

How we report: news from the last 24 hours plus what's coming up. Safety coverage sticks to notable incidents, and we never name private individuals. Protests and rallies are listed neutrally, for any cause. Photos come from the original source or free-licensed collections, always credited; if you own a photo and want it removed, tell us and it comes down.
