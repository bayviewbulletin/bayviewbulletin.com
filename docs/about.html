Bay View's morning paper

The Bay View Bulletin is a free daily guide to what's happening in Milwaukee's Bay View neighborhood, updated every morning by 6:30. It's a neighborhood project, not a company: no ads, no paywall and no paid placements.

Who's behind it

The Bulletin is an independent project run by a Bay View resident who serves as its editor: setting what we cover and how, deciding anything sensitive, and answering for every mistake. The easiest way to reach the editor is the tip form below.

How it's made

We don't have reporters at meetings or crime scenes. Each morning's edition is put together with the help of AI tools, which follow the editor's written rules. They read local news outlets, city and school sources, elected officials' announcements, public records, and the public social media posts of Bay View businesses and neighbors. Then they summarize what's new in our own words, link every item to its original source and credit every photo. Anything sensitive, uncertain or that could hurt someone is held back for the editor to review before it runs. If something here matters to you, follow the link and read the original reporting, and please support the outlets that do that work.

Where we cover

Bay View as neighbors know it, from the harbor and Jones Island south to Holt and Morgan avenues, and from I-43 east to Lake Michigan. Citywide news runs only when it directly affects Bay View.

Our standards

We run news from the last 24 hours plus what's coming up, and we don't repeat stories. Safety coverage sticks to notable incidents, and we never name private individuals. Protests and rallies are listed neutrally, for any cause. Elected officials are covered for what they do for Bay View, never for campaigns. The daily business spotlight is never paid for and is limited to independent, locally owned businesses. The Bay View Buzz rounds up public posts and labels rumors as unconfirmed.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it on the story with a note saying what changed, and list it on our corrections page. Photos come from the original source or free-licensed collections, always credited. If you own a photo and want it removed, tell us and it comes down.
