Wednesday, November 25, 2026

Spotted

Coyote spotted on second base, looking very relaxed

A neighbor walking three big dogs found a coyote lounging on second base at the Humboldt Park ball field near the playground around 9:45 Tuesday morning, apparently in no hurry to leave. It never approached anyone. She posted the heads-up for owners of small dogs and parents headed to the playground, and says the comment section, full of baseball jokes, is worth a read.

— Kara E. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
Hold the gravy

Ian's most popular pizza right now has stuffing and cranberry on it

Ian's Pizza says its Thanksgiving pie, loaded with turkey, stuffing, green beans, mushrooms, fried onions and cranberry sauce, is its most popular slice right now. It's around through Nov. 30, including at the R1VER shop on S. 1st Street. Just don't plan on it for Thursday itself: every Ian's location is closed on Thanksgiving Day.

— @ianspizzamke in Instagram · see the post →
Spooky season, part two

Brew-Jas goes full Nightmare Before Christmas this weekend

Brew-Jas Coffee House is turning its themed-weekend menu over to Jack Skellington. The lineup includes a Pumpkin King latte, a toasted-marshmallow What's This?, a blackberry Sally's Potion, a peppermint Zero's Christmas Wish and an Oogie Boogie Energy Red Bull refresher. The shop says it will also have vendors at both locations, including Howell Avenue, for Small Business Saturday.

— @brewjascoffeemke in Instagram · see the post →
UnconfirmedRave review

Reddit names Plume a top spot for women's resale

A r/milwaukee thread asking for the best women's clothing consignment shops got more than 30 replies, and Plume on KK earned a shout-out as "fantastic." The vintage boutique at 3001 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. is also running a Black Friday weekend sale starting Friday.

— r/milwaukee · see the post →
Thanksgiving Eve

Cafe Centraal stretches happy hour all night tonight

Centraal says its happy-hour deal, half off appetizers and taps, runs from 3 p.m. to close tonight for Thanksgiving Eve, the busiest bar night of the year. The KK cafe at 2306 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. calls it "too good to miss," which is the kind of thing a cafe would say.
