Spotted

Omen promises a 'Nutcracker Nightmare' for Small Business Saturday

Omen Alternative is turning its annual Small Business Saturday bash into a twisted Nutcracker, with staff in costume as rabid rodent royalty and sugar-addled fairies. It's the shop's first holiday blowout at its new 2649 S. KK home, with Goth Barge DJs, clearance deals and Record Store Day Black Friday releases. Doors open 10 a.m. Saturday, Nov. 28.

New in the oven

The Tuff Bones crew is now selling sourdough, too

The folks behind Tuff Bones Pet Supply, known for tattoo-style dog collars, announced they've finally caved to years of friends' urging and started a home bakery selling sourdough loaves to Bay View. Tuff Bones Bakery is a home kitchen operation; the post says the baker has been making loaves at least weekly for three years. Ordering details are in the post.

— Jameson V. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
Supper club scoop

Barnacle Bud's opens six bar seats to walk-ins on Wednesdays

Reservations-only no more, at least at the bar: starting Wednesday, Nov. 18, Barnacle Bud's winter supper club is holding its six bar seats for walk-ins every Wednesday through Dec. 30, first come, first served. Bar-only specials include a $30 steak frites and $9 brandy old fashioneds. Diners who bring food donations for the Bay View Community Center also get a free ice cream drink.

— @barnaclebuds in Instagram · see the post →
Overheard

Leave the leaves? The yard debate rages on as pickup gets extended

A shared post urging people to leave fallen leaves on their yards for pollinators and wildlife racked up nearly 200 reactions and dozens of comments in Bay View Town Hall. Fans called it free mulch; skeptics shared stories of critters and soggy lawns. Either way, the city's rake-out deadline is now Nov. 29.
