UnconfirmedRumor mill

Is Christmas on KK coming back? An organizer says yes

A neighbor asked whether Christmas on KK is happening this year, and a commenter who says he's organizing it answered that it's back and they're just getting organized, sharing a vendor application. One vendor chimed in that she'll be set up at the Magnet Factory on Dec. 11 from 5 to 9 p.m. We haven't seen an official announcement or full schedule yet, so treat the date as tentative.

— Carlene M. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
Ticket blitz

Every car on Logan Avenue woke up to a ticket, neighbors say

A neighbor posted Friday morning that every car parked on his stretch of Logan Avenue had a parking ticket, with no obvious reason why. The post drew nearly 100 comments, ranging from theories about revenue to people swapping stories of similar block-wide ticketing. No explanation from the city had surfaced by Saturday morning.

— Dave R. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
Best enemies

Lost Whale roasts Holey Mackerel, then signs up anyway

Lost Whale took a playful jab at its "best enemies" at Holey Mackerel, saying they're "actually doing something cool for once": a Merry Mackerel Holiday Village in Greenfield. Lost Whale says it's on board and is pitching local businesses on sponsorships due by Nov. 15. The fine print gives the feud away: the sponsor contact on the flyer has a Lost Whale email address.

— @lostwhalemke in Instagram · see the post →
Silent skies

The ground stop gave Bay View a rare quiet night

With every departure in the country grounded Friday, neighbors under the Mitchell flight path noticed the silence, and one joked that at least everyone would get a quiet night's sleep. Another asked whether there were ways to help stranded travelers, thinking of how Canadians took in stuck flyers in 2001. Flights resumed Friday evening; see our top story for travel tips.

— Amy T. and Stacey M. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
Deal alert

Ormson Supply clears the racks: $10 rack, $5 bins, cash only

Ormson Supply on KK is making room with a packed $10 clothing rack and two big bins of $5 clothes, open all weekend at regular hours. Bring cash: the clearance deals are cash only.
