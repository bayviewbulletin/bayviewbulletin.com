Friday, November 27, 2026

Crate digging

Acme Records has its Black Friday Record Store Day stash in

The KK shop posted every Record Store Day Black Friday title it ordered, along with a pile of new used arrivals, from vintage Japanese classic-rock pressings to a 10-LP Frank Zappa box set. The owners say they order what they think their regulars will buy, and thanked those regulars for helping them buy their building last year. Regular Friday hours are noon to 7 p.m.

Good deed

A Thanksgiving morning run raises nearly $1,000 for the BVCC pantry

Wild Workouts and Wellness on Delaware Avenue says its Wild Turkey Trot brought in nearly $1,000, plus bags of food, for the Bay View Community Center Food Pantry. A pantry volunteer posted in Bay View Town Hall that shelves were unusually full this week, fresh produce included.

— Wild Workouts and Wellness in Facebook and Instagram · see the post →
Spotted

Hand-made birch runes, made under the full moon, land at Strange Scene

A local maker posted a one-of-a-kind set of clay and birch-bark runes made under this week's full moon, headed for the shelves at Strange Scene. She says the materials can safely go back into the earth.

— @daughterof.odin in Instagram · see the post →
Hat check

McLaughlin & Hayes is buried in winter hat boxes

The KK hat shop says its Bailey winter stock has arrived, along with a few more Stetsons, and posted a photo of the unboxing aftermath piled across the shop floor. Cleanup, it says, is next.
