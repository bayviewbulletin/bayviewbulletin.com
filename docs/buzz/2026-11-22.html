Sunday, November 22, 2026

Scam alert

Got a payment request from the Bike Fed? Don't click it

The Wisconsin Bike Fed, headquartered on Becher Street, warned on Facebook this week that emails made to look like they come from one of its staff and asking for payments are a spoof or a hack. Its advice: don't click the link. The group says it's looking into it, so if you're signing up for the Dec. 5 Santa Cycle Rampage, go straight to its website instead of an emailed link.

Milestone

Swan Song turns one on Howell Avenue

Swan Song, the restaurant and bar at 3216 S. Howell Ave. that took over the old Sabrosa Cafe space, marked its first anniversary this week with a thank-you to everyone who's supported it. This week's featured soup is a Southwest-style chicken poblano.

— Swan Song in Facebook · see the post →
Thanksgiving plans

Patti's sets Thanksgiving hours: 3 p.m. to midnight

Nowhere to go Thursday? The tavern at 2800 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. says it will be open from 3 p.m. until midnight on Nov. 26, serving food (and its signature shots) for neighbors without other holiday plans.

— Patti's Power Plant in Facebook · see the post →
New on the menu

Centraal pours dirty sodas: Razzle Dazzle and Coconut Chaos

Centraal Grand Cafe & Tappery has added dirty sodas to its drink menus, starting with a raspberry-topped Razzle Dazzle and a creamy Coconut Chaos. The cafe calls them fizzy and fruity; the trend that took off out West has officially reached KK.

— Centraal Grand Cafe & Tappery (@cafecentraal) in Instagram · see the post →
UnconfirmedOverheard

Santa Rampage rookies ask Reddit what they're in for

A first-timer on r/milwaukee asked for tips before the Dec. 5 Santa Cycle Rampage. From what the search snippet shows, one reply suggests riding over via KK to the start and party, and packing a helmet, water and a lock. We couldn't read the full thread; the Bike Fed's own post says registration is open on its website.
