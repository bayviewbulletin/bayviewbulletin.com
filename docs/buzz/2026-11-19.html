Thursday, November 19, 2026

Settle this

La Salsa asks Bay View to name its one must-order dish

La Salsa on Oklahoma Avenue threw down a challenge to the neighborhood this week: what is the No. 1 thing to order there? The restaurant pitted its sizzling fajitas against its street tacos and more, and invited regulars to make their case in the comments.

— La Salsa (@lasalsamke) in Instagram · see the post →
UnconfirmedOverheard

Reddit's bookish drinkers have a favorite: Voyager

In an r/milwaukee thread on the best bars to read in, one commenter said they see a lot of people with books at Voyager, the wine bar on Lincoln Avenue. We only saw the Google snippet, so we can't say how the rest of the thread voted. Fittingly, Voyager is pouring free Beaujolais Nouveau samples tonight.

— u/Dry-Capital7005 in r/milwaukee · see the post →
Spotted

Lion's Tooth has signed copies of Gary Shteyngart's new novel

The KK bookshop and bar says it got signed copies of The Sensualist, Shteyngart's novel that came out this week. Its staff has started a weekly post on new releases and zines, and this week's also flags a Spanish graphic novel by Paco Roca and Rodrigo Terrasa.

— Lion's Tooth (@lionstoothmke) in Instagram · see the post →
Menu makeover

Crafty Cow says its Pig Pens got a glow-up

Crafty Cow on KK says its Pig Pens appetizer dates back a decade, but this year's version has been through what it calls a metamorphosis. The restaurant didn't spell out what changed, so a taste test may be in order.

— Crafty Cow (@craftycowwi) in Instagram · see the post →
Save the date

Cactus Club books a Valentine's Day show for 2027

Planning way ahead: Cactus Club announced that West Coast indie pop band Dummy will play the club on Sunday, Feb. 14, 2027, with Touch Girl Apple Blossom and Large Print. The all-ages show is $20 in advance and $25 at the door.

— Cactus Club (@cactusclubmke) in Instagram · see the post →
