Wednesday, November 18, 2026

Spotted

A Lime scooter takes a swim in the Humboldt Park pond

Somebody's ride ended badly: a neighbor posted photos of a Lime scooter sitting in the Humboldt Park lagoon, just off the dock, and asked if anyone wanted to fish it out. She promised to do it herself if it's still there by the weekend, while commenters suggested reporting it in the Lime app so the company sends someone.

— Ashley M. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
UnconfirmedRumor mill

Concoctions closed on Brady Street. Is Bay View next?

Concoctions, the Caribbean cocktail, boba and fruit-bouquet spot, quietly closed its Brady Street storefront Oct. 31, and its owner told Urban Milwaukee she hopes to land a new lease downtown or in Bay View. She's looking for a family-friendly space with a bigger kitchen for its slush-and-slime kids' parties. Nothing is signed yet, so call this one a wish list, not a done deal.

— Urban Milwaukee · see the post →
Good dog news

The Pup Stand on Oklahoma is now open all winter

Bay View dogs who tugged their humans to an empty spot last winter can relax: the neighbor behind a treat stand for pups on Oklahoma Avenue built a new, weatherproof version that goes out Thursday and will stay out all year. The post drew more than 100 reactions from grateful dog owners.

— Lori T. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
Last call

Buttah and Biscuit's Thanksgiving pre-orders close Friday

The biscuit shop in the Bay View Community Center kitchen says Thanksgiving orders for its biscuits, pies and desserts are open only through Friday, Nov. 20. It promises the biscuits will be the talk of the table, so plan accordingly.

— Buttah and Biscuit in Instagram · see the post →
New on the menu

Gordo's goes full tiramisu for its flavor of the month

Gordo's Bubble Waffles on Howell rolled out a tiramisu bubble waffle with tiramisu ice cream, a ladyfinger and espresso drizzle, plus a matching tiramisu latte and tiramisu mini pancakes. The shop calls it its favorite drop yet.
