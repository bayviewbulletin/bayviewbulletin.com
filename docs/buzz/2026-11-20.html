Friday, November 20, 2026

Curb alert

Free cookbooks on the curb: take two. Someone took 30.

A neighbor set out boxes of free cookbooks at Pine and Wilbur with a sign asking for two per person ("Santa is watching"), plus a donation box for MADACC pet supplies. Requests for specific titles poured in, and the poster later noted, with a sigh, that someone had walked off with a full box of about 30.

— Shannon M. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
Will you…?

A nervous note on a Tenuta's receipt: 'I am going to propose'

Tenuta's shared the scribble a diner slipped his server on Nov. 15, asking staff to distract his girlfriend for one second. She said yes. The restaurant says it has also hosted graduations, retirements and, yes, a gender reveal.

— Tenuta's Italian Restaurant (@tenutasbayview) in Instagram · see the post →
Vintage cred

Midwest Living names BC Modern Wisconsin's best vintage shop

Midwest Living's roundup of the best vintage shops in every Midwest state picked BC Modern, the once-a-month furniture sale on Chase Avenue, for Wisconsin. Its next weekend, the November Collection, is Nov. 28-29.

— BC Modern (@bcmodernmke) in Instagram · see the post →
Final bow

Spare A Pair's 15-year run ends with one big sale Saturday

The organizer behind Spare A Pair is hosting her last one Saturday, Nov. 21, at 11 a.m. at her home, with 89 handmade ponchos, cowboy hats, earrings and jean jackets made from recycled material, plus men's vintage clothes. Every dollar goes to helping people experiencing homelessness, and volunteers will assemble 100 lunches and 100-plus blessing bags. Cash or Venmo, adults only; details are in her post.

— Lori T. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
UnconfirmedRumor mill

No BVCC holiday gift match this year?

A neighbor asked whether the Bay View Community Center is running its holiday gift match for kids again, and a commenter said she'd been told it isn't. We haven't confirmed that with the center. Neighbors pointed to the Salvation Army's Angel Tree and Project Concern's giving tree in St. Francis as alternatives.

— Anonymous in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
Thanksgiving in a bun

Vanguard stuffs Thanksgiving into a turducken sausage

Vanguard's new special is a turkey, duck and chicken sausage on a bun. It comes with herb-cranberry cream cheese, house stuffing, onion gravy and fried onions. Basically the whole holiday plate, no fork required.

— Vanguard (@vanguardmilwaukee) in Instagram · see the post →
Comfort carbs

Egg & Flour makes gnocchi inspired by a KFC Famous Bowl

The Howell Avenue pasta shop's latest special is potato gnocchi with a gravy cream sauce, roasted corn, cheddar and fried chicken. Egg & Flour pitched it as the dish for days when you choose comfort food over the gym.

— Egg & Flour Pasta Bar (@eggandflourmke) in Instagram · see the post →
Drop and give 'em 20

Club Garibaldi offers a free shot for push-up attempts

Garibaldi opens at 11 a.m. Saturday for the Badgers-Purdue game. Anyone who attempts push-ups, Bucky-style, gets a free shot. The grill shuts down at 4 p.m. before that night's blues show.

— Club Garibaldi (@clubgaribaldi) in Instagram · see the post →
