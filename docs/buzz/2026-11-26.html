Straight talk

Lion's Tooth: still working second jobs, still asking you to shop small

The KK bookstore's owners posted their yearly holiday letter, calling the next 30 days a make-or-break stretch for indie businesses and saying they still work second jobs to keep the shop going. They also added a staffer and posted longer holiday hours starting Dec. 10, including a 9 a.m. to noon window on Christmas Day. The shop is closed today.

Crafty

Make a lantern at Chippy's, then catch 'Hexed' at the Avalon

Chippy's is pairing up with the Avalon for Black Friday: a Hexed-themed lantern-making session at the shop from 2:15 to 3:15 p.m. ahead of the 3:30 showing, and again from 5 to 6 p.m. before the 6:30 show. The two storefronts are a few doors apart on KK.

— Chippy's (@shopchippysmke) in Instagram · see the post →
Deal alert

Ela Mari's Black Friday sale starts online today

The KK boutique is giving online shoppers a Thanksgiving Day head start on its 30%-off-everything sale. The store itself opens Friday from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. with the same discount, plus $20 in store credit for purchases of $125 or more, good Dec. 1-15.

— Ela Mari Boutique (@elamariboutique) in Instagram · see the post →
Sweet tooth

Gordo's is pushing warm cookie dough under ice cream

The Howell Avenue dessert shop is promoting a cup of freshly baked, scooped cookie dough topped with ice cream, billing it as its take on a viral treat. Gordo's is best known for its bubble waffles.
