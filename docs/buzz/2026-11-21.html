Saturday, November 21, 2026

Overheard

Snow-removal waitlists already? In a record-warm November

A neighbor two blocks north of Humboldt Park went looking for snow-removal referrals because the company they usually hire already has a waitlist this year. The job is no small one, either: a duplex on a hill with front stairs, two porches, garage paths and four garbage and recycling carts to dig out. Friday hit 67 degrees, but around here, planning ahead is the Wisconsin way.

— Mandy M. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
Deal alert

Buy a bike at Wheel & Sprocket, take home a Christmas tree

Wheel & Sprocket says it's "branching out": buy any bike at its Bay View store, or its Brookfield, Fox Point, Franklin or Delafield shops, and you get a free Christmas tree. Fair warning that strapping a spruce to a new bike is not covered in the owner's manual.

— @wheelandsprocket in Instagram · see the post →
On the menu

17th Ward's new cocktail is called Side Piece. There's duck fat in it.

The KK cocktail bar rolled out a five-drink "Fall Intermission" menu this week, and the headliner is Side Piece: apple brandy washed with duck fat, plus butternut squash and a brown sugar-sage syrup. Thanksgiving dinner in a glass, more or less.

— @17thwardmke in Instagram · see the post →
UnconfirmedSpotted

Rush Creek Reserve sighting at Outpost Bay View

In an r/milwaukee thread hunting for Rush Creek Reserve, the spruce-wrapped, spoonable seasonal cheese from Wisconsin's Uplands Cheese, one commenter said they'd seen it at Outpost's Bay View store about a week earlier. We couldn't confirm what's on the shelf today, so call the KK store before you make a special trip.

— r/milwaukee in Reddit · see the post →
Mark your calendar

The Avalon plans a maker market movie night Dec. 4

The Avalon says it's hosting a pop-up maker market with a movie night on Friday, Dec. 4, with local vendors Good Shit Video, Chippy's, Lion's Tooth and 4 of 4 Creative. Tickets are on sale through the theater; it's on our Events page.

— Avalon Atmospheric Theater in Facebook and Instagram · see the post →
New on the menu

EsterEv adds an everyday, drop-in menu

EsterEv says it now has a regular menu built for walk-ins: the kind of night where you grab a few plates and a drink on a random Friday without making it a whole event. One of the new plates pairs rye toast with apple and shaved radish.

— @esterev.mke in Instagram · see the post →
The hunt

Lefse before Thanksgiving: where does Bay View buy it?

With the holiday coming, a neighbor asked the group where to find lefse, the soft Norwegian potato flatbread that shows up on a lot of Midwest holiday tables. Commenters pointed toward a big-box grocer outside the neighborhood, so if a Bay View shop carries it, now's the time to speak up.
