Rave review

Bay View shops are all over OnMilwaukee's holiday gift guides

OnMilwaukee's two new gift guides read like a stroll down KK. URSA alone shows up again and again, for snail-mucin face masks, butter-stick and croissant hair clips and a dried sourdough starter, while Semolina gets nods for caviar-tin candles, Sicilian olive oils and Spanish tinned fish. BC Modern on Chase made the boutique list too; the shop itself says this month's sale runs Saturday and Sunday, Nov. 28 and 29, 10 to 4.

Feel-good

Brew-Jas thanks the neighbors behind its turkey giveaway

Brew-Jas Coffee House says this year's turkey giveaway happened only because friends and fellow small businesses showed up big. The Howell Avenue coffee shop handed out turkeys on Sunday at 3062 S. 13th St., one per family, and has posted a thank-you to its sponsors. The crew says giving back is something it looks forward to every year.

Neighbors

One neighbor's Thanksgiving meal drive is down to its last delivery

A neighbor who has been collecting donations to cook Thanksgiving meals for families in St. Francis and Bay View says nearly every requested meal has gone out, with just one delivery left. In a Bay View Town Hall post, he thanked the donors who chipped in money, groceries and seasoning, and the volunteers who did the shopping.

Just booked

Cactus Club lands viral comedian Dan Carney for March

Cactus Club has announced a stand-up show by Dan Carney, a New York comedian known for his viral videos, on Monday, March 22, at 7 p.m. Tickets are on sale now: $20 in advance, $25 at the door.

South Shore Yacht Club's lake view makes the Great Lakes photo page

A shot of Lake Michigan taken from the South Shore Yacht Club in Bay View popped up this week on r/GreatLakesPics, Reddit's gallery of Great Lakes scenery. We could only see the title and a snippet, so we can't vouch for the details, but the view from the end of the South Shore breakwater rarely disappoints.
