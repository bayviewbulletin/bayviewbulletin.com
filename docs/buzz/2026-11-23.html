Only in Bay View

A possum rescue wants your freezer-burned meat

Cleaning out the freezer before the Thanksgiving groceries arrive? A neighbor who collects food for a possum rescue says she'll be parked outside the Starbucks at KK and Oklahoma on Sunday, Nov. 29, from noon to 4, with a cooler on the sidewalk for expired or freezer-burned meat, seafood, cheese, veggies and fruit (no spices, please). Got a full grocery bag or more? She'll arrange a pickup. The burning question in the comments: will an actual possum be there?

— Karen W. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
Game on

Busby's Holiday Bingo returns, starting Thanksgiving Eve

The corner bar's seasonal bingo card is back, with new squares and new events this year. Stamps start Wednesday at its Last Waltz night; squares range from wearing an ugly sweater to reciting a holiday movie line, and a full card earns a $20 Busby's gift certificate. Busby's pitches it as a gift to yourself if nobody else fills your stocking with beer.

Want to pass as a local? Never say 'Kinnickinnic'

A r/milwaukee thread asked what would instantly blow a non-local's cover, like the bar scene in Inglourious Basterds. Among the answers: pronouncing the L in Milwaukee, and saying Kinnickinnic instead of KK. We only saw this in a search snippet, but anyone who's given directions on our main drag knows the rule.

Pour decision

New Barons' Pecan Porter is back for Black Friday

The brewing co-op says its Pecan Porter returns for Black Friday, with a toasty, nutty nose and a pecan-pie malt backbone. At 7.5% ABV, the brewers call it boozy enough for the occasion but tame enough to keep the party going.

Hats off

McLaughlin & Hayes takes its couture hats to Chicago

The Bay View hat shop says its atelier is busy making one-of-a-kind headwear for its debut at Chicago's One of a Kind Holiday Show, Dec. 3-6. First look: a boater trimmed in blue silk roses. Back home, it's closed on Thanksgiving.
