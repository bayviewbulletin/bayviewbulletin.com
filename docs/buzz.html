Rumor confirmed

It IS the old Dobie's: The Hideout opens Tuesday

Milwaukee Record's Tyler Maas tipped the group to The Hideout, a speakeasy-themed bar and grill opening 'just across the border,' and a commenter guessed the old Dobie's. Guess confirmed: it's at 4136 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. in St. Francis, the longtime Dobie's Steak House spot, opening at noon Tuesday, Nov. 17.

— Tyler M. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
Burger beef

Nite Owl's last call sparks a cheeseburger civil war

The South Side drive-in is wrapping its 2026 season 'sometime this week,' per a Milwaukee Record post shared to the group, and that's all it took. One camp wants it shut down for good; another shrugged that a plain cheeseburger was perfectly fine. Get one before the spring reopening, or stay out of the comments.

— Tyler M. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →

Coyote seen strolling a cemetery

A neighbor posted a coyote sighting in a local cemetery with a warning to watch small pets. Commenters pushed back that coyotes mostly want rabbits and rodents, and one noticed it was hanging out near her own family's plot.

Mystery solved

What WAS in Collector's Edge's windows?

Bay View's comic shop at 2330 S. KK used to fill its windows with giant comic-cover posters, and a Milwaukee Record look-back shared to the group finally explained them. A former Collector's Edge employee chimed in with the backstory; the remaining question is why the faded ones stay up.

— Tyler M. in Bay View Town Hall (Facebook) · see the post →
Overheard

'Has anyone actually tried The Bad Piggy?'

A neighbor admitted driving past the Puerto Rican spot on Howell 'hundreds of times' without noticing it. For the record: the former food truck put down roots at 4068 S. Howell Ave., the old Campbell's Irish Pub, and the comments are filling up with plate recommendations.

Dog spa takes on the pup that flunked the test

A Great Pyrenees owner gave Dog City Hotel & Spa (1775 E. Bolivar Ave., just over the line in St. Francis) a loud shout-out: after years of grooming the famously hard-to-groom breed, staff agreed to board him next week even though he 'failed their temperament testing miserably.'

Packer Chad rolls out a bowling fundraiser for vets

Clementine's 'Packer Chad' is hosting a first annual bowling event at Bay View Bowl on Sun., Nov. 22, at 1 p.m., with profits going to the Milwaukee Homeless Veterans Initiative. A few lanes are still open and he's collecting raffle prizes.
