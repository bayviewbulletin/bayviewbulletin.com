Friday, November 13, 2026

Sticker shock

Did your city bill just jump? Neighbors say theirs did

A post asking whether anyone else's city water bill shot up drew more than 100 comments, with several neighbors saying theirs ran about $50 higher than usual. Some pointed to the once-a-year snow and ice charge, though at least one said the jump showed up even without it. We didn't find a new rate hike announced this fall; the city's posted 2026 charges list the snow and ice fee at $1.19 per foot of street frontage, billed once a year, plus a quarterly solid waste charge of $67.95.

A 600-foot freighter is waiting off the harbor

A Reddit photo of the cargo ship Verila, anchored off Milwaukee's harbor at sunrise, had people asking why. The poster's take: ships sometimes wait offshore rather than pay to tie up at the dock. If you've seen a big ship sitting still off South Shore, that's likely the one.

Fancy that

Heirloom's 'EBT & Fine China' dinner is Sunday

Heirloom MKE on Howell says tickets are still available for EBT & Fine China, a one-night tasting dinner with guest chef Q. Ibraheem built around ingredients associated with getting by, served with fine-dining care. Urban Milwaukee lists it for Sunday, Nov. 15, 5 to 8 p.m., at $140 a person, with $5 from each ticket going to the food-insecurity nonprofit Resources & Beyond.
