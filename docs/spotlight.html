Sunday, November 15, 2026

Lion's Tooth

Zines, comics and small-press books, plus a tiny bar and book clubs: Bay View's punk-spirited bookstore.

Lion's Tooth is a bookstore and art space at 2421 S. Kinnickinnic Ave., in a small storefront that used to be a tattoo parlor. It specializes in zines, prints, small-press books, underground comics and graphic novels for kids and adults, with a mini gallery on the walls and a cozy bar where you can get a drink while you browse.

The shop is run by co-owners Cris Siqueira, a Brazilian-born filmmaker and multimedia artist, and Shelly McClone-Carriere, who helped found the Riverwest Co-op Grocery & Café. They started Lion's Tooth in 2019 as a subscription service and pop-up, then hunted for a storefront during the pandemic and opened the KK shop in March 2021, when customers were only allowed just inside the front door. The name comes from the Portuguese word for dandelion, dente de leão, a plant the owners liked for its knack for surviving and spreading.

The owners call it a mission-driven bookstore: much of what's on the shelves comes from local and independent artists, musicians, poets and printmakers you won't find in big stores. McClone-Carriere once described it as the record store of bookstores. Events are the heart of the place: themed book clubs on Thursday evenings, author talks, artist mixers, a cartoonist residency and a Comics School for kids and teens that also raises money for the store. Every event is free.

The shop celebrated five years on KK in March with a benefit show at Cactus Club featuring the owners' own bands, and it won a 2025 Shepherd Express Best of Milwaukee award. Local TV has profiled it too, from CBS 58's look at how indie booksellers compete with Amazon to TMJ4's story on its artist residency.

What's new: a staff "news flash" this week highlighted fresh arrivals, including the new comics diary My Summer Diary, and the shop just hosted a book release party for Peter Dahl's Lives We Might Have Lived. Next up is Small Business Saturday on Nov. 28. Note the holiday hours: closed Thanksgiving, then open late (to 8 p.m. most days) starting Dec. 10. Book clubs take December off.

Lion's Tooth, 2421 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. Open every day: Mon.-Wed. 11 a.m.-5 p.m., Thu.-Fri. 11-6, Sat. 10-6, Sun. 10-5. lionstoothmke.com, @lionstoothmke on Instagram.
