Friday, November 27, 2026

Sugar Maple

A former bank, post office and Sikh temple on Lincoln Avenue, pouring 60 drafts since 2008

Sugar Maple is a craft beer bar and event space at 441 E. Lincoln Ave., in the middle of Bay View's Lincoln Avenue business strip. Its website lists 60 beers on draft, with a focus on small and local breweries, plus craft cocktails made with local spirits and a range of non-alcoholic drinks. There is no kitchen and no TV; Urban Milwaukee's 2019 Bar Exam column noted that fans of "a decor of beer and professional sports will be disappointed."

The bar opened on April 1, 2008. It was started by Adrienne Pierluissi and Bruno Johnson, who had opened their first bar, the Palm Tavern, in 2003, and it is named for Wisconsin's state tree. Pierluissi is listed as the sole owner in state business filings and in the bar's 2023 anniversary announcement. The original idea was beer brewed only in North America; since 2015 the bar has concentrated on small breweries, as local as possible, including brand-new ones looking for a first audience.

The building has a long history of its own. It went up in 1918 as a brick office building described in city files as a post office, then served as a bank, a credit union and, from 1999 to 2007, the Sikh Temple of Wisconsin. The restrooms sit in the old bank vault, behind 16-inch concrete walls. Pierluissi designed the curved bar top and painted the large artworks on the walls, according to a 2013 Urban Milwaukee profile, which also reported that the Shepherd Express named its beer selection the city's best in 2009.

Over the years the bar has added beer releases, pop-up dinners, live music, art shows, comedy, theater and fundraisers to the calendar. It is known for themed pop-ups, several with Milwaukee Record, such as a 2024 tribute to comedian Joe Pera and an It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia bar takeover. Since 2021, bartenders Harrison Browning and Xay Matabele have built the cocktail list around house-made syrups, bitters and shrubs.

No special events are posted for the holiday weekend, so expect regular hours. Next week brings Hablar Con Amigos, a judgment-free Spanish practice night, on Wednesday, Dec. 2 at 7 p.m., followed on Thursday, Dec. 3 by monthly mahjong at 7 p.m. and a ticketed show by Haunted Like Human at 7:30 p.m. Later in December: Drag King Bingo (Dec. 7), the Underground Science Society discussion night (Dec. 14), a five-act Mink Ranch Presents bill (Dec. 19) and Frogwater (Dec. 20).

Sugar Maple, 441 E. Lincoln Ave. Open Mon.-Thurs. 4-11 p.m., Fri. 4 p.m.-2 a.m., Sat. noon-2 a.m., Sun. noon-10 p.m. mysugarmaple.com, Instagram @thesugarmaple.
