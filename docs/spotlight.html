Sunday, November 22, 2026

BC Modern

Bay View's once-a-month vintage showroom fills a former credit union with fresh mid-century finds

BC Modern is a vintage furniture shop at 3116 S. Chase Ave. with a twist: it opens only one weekend a month. Each sale fills two floors and about 5,000 square feet with mid-century modern and vintage furniture, lighting, decor and collectibles, and every weekend brings an entirely new collection.

It's run by Eric and Veronica Lewis, who describe it as Milwaukee's largest family-operated Black- and Brown-owned vintage store. The shop grew out of their auction businesses: We Bay Auctions, an eBay consignment service started in 2005, and Brew City Auctions, a full-service estate liquidation company added in 2011. BC Modern followed in 2015. In 2021 they bought the Chase Avenue building, built in the 1960s as Nordberg Credit Union, and FOX6 covered the grand reopening there in September 2022 after about six months of renovation.

Most pieces come straight from private estates and collectors. Only the best make the showroom floor; the rest go through the couple's live and online auctions. The shop says everything is cleaned, and refinished or reupholstered when needed, before it's priced. Expect sectionals, credenzas, lamps, barware and glass, plus CeCe's Candy Shop, a nostalgic candy counter on the garden level.

The once-a-month format draws a crowd: Shepherd Express reported in May that more than a thousand shoppers come through on a sale weekend. This month, Midwest Living picked BC Modern as Wisconsin's best vintage shop in its state-by-state roundup. The business also buys estates, offers styling services and sells gift cards and a VIP membership with early access.

Its recent posts preview the November Collection: a magenta sectional, a couch and chair in fresh upholstery, vintage Christmas decor and glowing ceramic trees. The sale runs Saturday and Sunday, Nov. 28 and 29, with VIP early access Friday evening. December's sale moves up to Dec. 12-13 because of the holidays.

BC Modern, 3116 S. Chase Ave. Open the last full weekend of each month (Dec. 12-13 in December), Saturday and Sunday 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. bcmodernmke.com, @bcmodernmke on Instagram.
