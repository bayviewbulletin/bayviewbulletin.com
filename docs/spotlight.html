Heirloom MKE

The Howell Avenue restaurant grew out of a yellow food truck, survived a fire and still serves its famous burrata

Heirloom MKE is a farm-to-table restaurant and bar at 2378 S. Howell Ave., in the storefront that was home to Lazy Susan for nine years. Nearly everything is made from scratch with ingredients from local farms and purveyors, and the menu marks which items are locally sourced and which can be made vegan or gluten-free.

It's run by Pete and Jess Ignatiev, a husband-and-wife team who describe themselves as chef and gardener. Both are restaurant-industry veterans, and they were close to opening a restaurant in early 2020 when the pandemic hit. Instead they launched Heirloom that year as a food truck, and its upscale comfort food built a following at breweries and night markets around town.

The road to Howell Avenue had a scary detour. In March 2023 the couple announced they would take over the Lazy Susan space, and in July, weeks before opening, an early-morning fire destroyed the truck while it was parked in Bay View. No one was hurt. The restaurant opened anyway on Aug. 26, 2023, in a dining room with a jade-green ceiling and wallpaper that, if you look closely, shows alien abductions.

The hand-stretched burrata salad that made the truck famous is still on the menu, along with fried cheese curds, the Heirloom burger, grilled shrimp polenta and a vegan turmeric-and-ginger cauliflower steak. The list changes with the seasons and leans on Wisconsin beers, wine and a short list of creative cocktails. Friday is fish fry night: two pieces of beer-battered cod with fries, coleslaw, tartar sauce and buttered rye.

The truck story has a happy ending, too. In July 2025, almost two years after the fire, a new yellow truck the owners call Lucille 2 hit the road; their post announcing it said "our little yellow truck is BACK." Heirloom now runs both the restaurant and the truck, and its website lists where the truck will be.

Heirloom MKE, 2378 S. Howell Ave. Open Wednesday through Saturday, 4 to 9 p.m. (closed Sunday through Tuesday); reservations recommended, walk-ins welcome. heirloommke.com, Instagram @heirloom.mke.
