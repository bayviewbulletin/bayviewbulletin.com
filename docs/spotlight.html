Saturday, November 14, 2026

Cactus Club

From a 1950s country bar to the room where the White Stripes played: Bay View's tiny, all-ages music landmark.

Cactus Club is a small music venue and bar on a residential corner at S. Wentworth and E. Russell avenues. Its stage room holds about 200 people and books bands almost every night, most of them all ages, while the front bar keeps its own calendar of DJ happy hours, karaoke and club meetups.

The building is much older than the club. OnMilwaukee's Urban Spelunking series traced a tavern there to at least 1907, and found that police raided an Italian anarchist circle that met inside in 1917. Cliff and Alice Rose turned it into a country-western bar called the Cactus Club in the late 1950s (the club's own history says 1957; OnMilwaukee says 1958), which explains the name. Eric Uecke took over in 1996 and built it into one of the city's essential indie and punk rooms. Kelsey Kaufmann, who started working there in 2011 and ran the booking from 2016, bought the club in February 2020, just weeks before the pandemic shut music venues down.

The roster of bands that played the room on the way up is long. The club lists the White Stripes, Queens of the Stone Age, Death Cab for Cutie, Interpol, Bright Eyes and Low, and Milwaukee Record has reported that the White Stripes played their first out-of-state show there in 1999. In January 2020 it became the first Milwaukee venue to use a new all-ages license, a rule change it had pushed for with Girls Rock Camp.

Under Kaufmann the club has also become a community space through Cactus+, an arts nonprofit started in 2022: open decks where anyone can DJ, a knitting club, queer karaoke, a book club, workshops and markets. It rebuilt its front entrance for wheelchair access, too. Jack White played a surprise fundraiser there in October 2024 and, according to Milwaukee Record and WPR, gave $20,000 in 2025 to cover a city-required curb bump-out. The finished ramp includes a mosaic by artist Kate Klingbeil.

This weekend brings a free Open Decks session today at 1 p.m., two bills tonight, and Sunday's free Milwaukee Record Halftime Show at 11:30 a.m. Squirrel Flower and Sour Widows play Thursday. The club also just announced that its Cactus Winter Market returns Sunday, Dec. 13, from noon to 4 p.m., with vendors to be revealed.

Cactus Club, 2496 S. Wentworth Ave. Open Monday and Tuesday 6 p.m. to midnight; Wednesday and Thursday 4 p.m. to midnight; Friday 4 p.m. to 2:30 a.m.; Saturday 1 p.m. to 2:30 a.m.; Sunday 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. cactusclubmilwaukee.com, @cactusclubmke on Instagram.
