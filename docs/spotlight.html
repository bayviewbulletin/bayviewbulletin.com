Friday, November 20, 2026

Burnhearts

A Logan Avenue tavern with a long past, an aged-beer stash and the February block party that fills the street

Burnhearts is a dimly lit corner tavern at 2599 S. Logan Ave., a few blocks east of Kinnickinnic Avenue on a mostly residential stretch of Bay View. It is a beer bar first: when Urban Milwaukee visited in 2013 it counted 24 taps and two casks, and the bar's website still keeps separate draft, bottle-and-can and cocktail lists.

William "B.J." Seidel and Jess Seidel bought the building in October 2006, spent about eight months renovating it and opened on June 8, 2007. The name honors William's great-grandmother. The corner has poured drinks for a long time. Before Burnhearts it was Carl's, a mid-century supper club, then Suds, O'Brien's Call Box and Flanigan's Call Box. Neighborhood lore says it was a brothel and gambling parlor during Prohibition, a story Urban Milwaukee warned might be embellished. A 2016 Bar Exam column traced the tavern to the 1890s or earlier.

Regulars come for the beer. William Seidel ages imperial stouts, barley wines and sours in a walk-in cooler and releases them on a list called Carl's Private Stash, named after the old supper club. Brandy has a following too. The draft list on the bar's website includes Central Waters' barrel-aged Brandyland Old Fashioned. In July 2018 the Seidels opened a back patio, a project they had planned since about a year after opening and set aside during the recession. It has a gate built by JR's Welding & Fabrication and a floral mural by bartender Mic Makalinao.

The bar is best known around town for Mitten Fest, a February block party on the street outside the tavern, which it has run since 2013 for Hunger Task Force. Milwaukee Magazine reported that it drew nearly 10,000 people. When COVID-19 canceled the 2021 party, the bar sold bottled cocktails to go on Sundays as "Miss You Fest" and gave part of the proceeds to the food bank. For 2024 the owners passed the party to Boone & Crockett so they could take a year to "re-envision" their own space. It came back to Logan Avenue on Feb. 7, 2026, with The Hallelujah Ward headlining. Each summer the bar also hosts the Burnhearts/Pabst Street Party, which has run for more than a decade.

Burnhearts' own Instagram has been quiet since a late-October post, and it hasn't announced anything for this week. Its back room still draws music, though: in late August, people who were there posted photos and video of a set by Promise Ring singer Davey von Bohlen. Watch @burnhearts this winter for the next Mitten Fest date and lineup.

Burnhearts, 2599 S. Logan Ave. Open Mon.–Fri. 3 pm–2 am and Sat.–Sun. noon–2 am, per its website. burnheartsbar.com · @burnhearts on Instagram
