This weekend in Bay View

It's a shop-small kind of weekend in Bay View: Black Friday sales on KK, a long list of Small Business Saturday specials, and holiday pop-ups kicking off. Save room for music on Friday night and a free community dinner on Sunday.

Our picks

Sat. Nov. 28 · 10 am-6 pm

Small Business Saturday at URSA

URSA, 2534 S. Kinnickinnic Ave.. URSA anchors the KK shop-small day with 10% off and goodie-filled totes, stocked by 30-plus local businesses, for its first 50 big spenders.

Sun. Nov. 29 · 2:30-3:30 pm

Free Thanksgiving meal at Bay View United Methodist

Bay View United Methodist Church, 2772 S. Kinnickinnic Ave.. A free sit-down Thanksgiving dinner for anyone who wants one, no questions asked.
