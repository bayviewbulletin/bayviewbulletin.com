This weekend in Bay View

The holiday season starts creeping into Bay View this weekend, with a small-business block party on Delaware Avenue and chai nog back on the menu at Twisted Path. In between there's a full slate of all-ages shows, a free library conversation and a couple of good causes.

Our picks

Sat. Nov. 21 · 11 am

The Frozen Chair Market

3044 S. Delaware Ave.. Saturday's small-business block party with Component Brewing is a chance to start holiday shopping close to home.

Sat. Nov. 21 · 6:30 pm

Humbird, Bad Posture Club + Chris Rosenau

Cactus Club, 2496 S. Wentworth Ave.. Milwaukee Record's pick for Saturday: Humbird brings songs from its fourth album to an all-ages Cactus Club show, $10 in advance.

Sat. Nov. 21 · 1 pm

Death Café

Bay View Library, 2566 S. Kinnickinnic Ave.. A free, unhurried Saturday-afternoon conversation at the library about life, death and everything in between.

Sun. Nov. 22 · 12 pm

DIY Beaded Necklace Workshop

Cactus Club, 2496 S. Wentworth Ave.. An all-ages Sunday drop-in where kids and adults can string a necklace together from a big bead collection.

Sun. Nov. 22 · 1 pm

Clementine’s bowling fundraiser for homeless vets

Bay View Bowl, 2416 S. Kinnickinnic Ave.. Bowl a few frames at Bay View Bowl on Sunday and the profits go to the Milwaukee Homeless Veterans Initiative.
