County golf courses, including nearby Grant Park, stay open through at least Sunday

The mild weather is giving golfers extra rounds. Milwaukee County Parks is keeping eight courses open through at least Sunday, Nov. 22, Urban Milwaukee reported Wednesday.

The full-size courses at Brown Deer, Dretzka, Lincoln, Oakwood and Grant parks are taking tee-time reservations. Grant Park, in South Milwaukee, is the closest to Bay View. The par-3 courses at Noyes, Lake and Zablocki parks are open too. Parks says the season could run longer if conditions hold.

The extra weeks help the whole park system: golf brought in about 23% of the Parks budget in 2025. County courses have logged more than 390,000 rounds and more than $12 million in revenue so far in 2026, both records.
