After the Burrell Street shooting, city orders the party rental to get licensed

The home on the 2400 block of S. Burrell Street where a weekend party preceded Sunday's early-morning shooting never had the tourist rooming house license Milwaukee requires for stays shorter than a month, the Department of Neighborhood Services told TMJ4. DNS has ordered the owner to get and pay for a license by Dec. 16 and separately ordered the property to register with the city. An 18-year-old suffered non-life-threatening gunshot wounds; police have not announced an arrest.

Ald. Marina Dimitrijevic said in a Facebook post Monday that police told her office a party of 20 or more people was using the rental. Her office asked for, and the city added, a way to report a possibly unlicensed short-term rental through the city's online service request system (city.milwaukee.gov/ucc/mkemobile), and it has also reported the incident to the rental platform.

A representative for the owner told TMJ4 the booking was meant for four people and that the owner is working with the city. Anyone with information about the shooting can call MPD at 414-935-7360 or Crime Stoppers at 414-224-TIPS.
