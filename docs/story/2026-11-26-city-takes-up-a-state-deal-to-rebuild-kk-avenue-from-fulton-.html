City takes up a state deal to rebuild KK Avenue from Fulton Street to Lapham Boulevard

Two resolutions introduced at City Hall on Nov. 24 would line the city up with the Wisconsin Department of Transportation to rebuild S. Kinnickinnic Avenue, the state highway (WIS 32) that runs through the middle of Bay View. One covers about 1.35 miles from Fulton Street north to E. Becher Street; the other picks up at Becher and continues to Lapham Boulevard. Both were sent to the Common Council's Public Works Committee.

The state-city agreement for the Fulton-to-Becher section, dated Oct. 29, estimates the whole project at $23.1 million. Federal and state money would cover about $21 million and the city about $2.05 million. Construction is pegged at $19.55 million, including $13.5 million for the roadway itself, with $2.8 million for design and $500,000 for buying land. The Becher-to-Lapham file sets up $1.85 million for design and $200,000 for land; it doesn't list a construction estimate.

The agreement doesn't yet say what the rebuilt street would look like: the nature of the work is to be "determined by project scoping." Because the state treats parking lanes as a local cost, the city would pay the 13% of the paving bill tied to parking, plus items such as adjusting water and sewer covers.

No construction date is listed in either file. The Public Works Committee hasn't scheduled them yet; Bay View's alderperson is Marina Dimitrijevic (District 14).
