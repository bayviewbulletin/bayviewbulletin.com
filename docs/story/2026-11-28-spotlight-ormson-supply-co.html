Spotlight: Ormson Supply Co.

Ormson Supply Co. is a vintage shop at 2866 S. Kinnickinnic Ave., on the corner storefront with the big plate-glass windows (and, often, a taxidermied deer standing guard). It sells mid-century modern furniture, much of it Scandinavian, along with records, clothing, housewares and whatever else the owners think is worth hauling home.

The business started as Brew City Salvage, which owner Caleb Nickel ran out of his home before opening storefronts in Waukesha and then on National Avenue in Walker's Point. Nickel, a former truck driver who also ran a window-cleaning business, told the Shepherd Express that rummaging with his mom as a kid is "ingrained in my DNA." His cousin Ross Severson, a mixed-media artist who runs the vintage housewares collection 414 Modern, became his business partner.

In September 2020 the shop moved to Bay View, a move Nickel told Urban Milwaukee he had wanted for years, and took a new name. Ormson is a family name: both cousins' mothers were Ormsons, and the Norwegian roots suit a store heavy on Scandinavian pieces. The black-and-white logo is the family's bumerke, an old Norwegian mark once used to label property. Nickel also said the old name kept bringing in calls from people hunting for car parts.

What you'll find changes constantly, because the owners are out sourcing pieces most days of the week. Nickel has said he's partial to handmade furniture over mass-produced pieces, and the shop has held monthly weekend "Small Sales" on smaller items. Customers can also ask about pieces they see on the shop's Facebook and Instagram pages.

This week the shop posted a video tour of new stock and said it will be open 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. on Small Business Saturday, with a nudge to support small shops, many of which it says need the help this year.

Ormson Supply Co., 2866 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. Open Saturday, Nov. 28, 11 a.m.-4 p.m.; check its Facebook or Instagram (@ormsonsupply) for other hours.
