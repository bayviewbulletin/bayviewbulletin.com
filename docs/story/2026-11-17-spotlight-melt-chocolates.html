Spotlight: Melt Chocolates

Inside the Lincoln Warehouse at 2018 S. 1st St., behind a window that looks straight into the kitchen, Melt Chocolates turns out small batches of glossy, brightly painted bonbons. Its small factory store has been open in Bay View since 2017.

Melt is the work of Bjorn Rogness, who grew up in Door County and came to Milwaukee to study engineering at MSOE. He cooked his way through school, including years on the line at Ristorante Bartolotta. In 2008, in the middle of the Great Recession, he and business partner David answered a Craigslist ad for used chocolate equipment, cleaned it up and made their first batch on New Year's Eve. The company grew from a home kitchen to a shared commercial kitchen and then a kitchen in New Berlin before signing a lease in Bay View and opening the storefront in fall 2017. Rogness has kept a full-time engineering career alongside it, and he told OnMilwaukee that "chocolate is far more technical than people might think."

That technical streak shows in the shop's European tempering equipment, a climate-controlled kitchen and a display case built to keep filled chocolates fresh. Melt is known for bold flavor pairings: its Calypso truffle combines mango, habanero and Jamaican rum in milk chocolate ganache. Rogness, who has Type 1 diabetes, has said that shaped the shop's commitment to chocolates without preservatives or artificial ingredients.

Calypso won a gold medal from London's Academy of Chocolate in 2023, with the vegan blueberry-cinnamon-cardamom Kashmir taking silver. Melt added three more medals in 2025: silver for Mascarpone Limon and bronze for Passion Fruit and Red Raspberry. Close to home, it has been a lead sponsor of Bay View Gallery Night since 2019 and underwrites WMSE radio.

This season brings Melt's first Advent calendar, a limited run of 200 with 24 holiday flavors such as eggnog, figgy pudding and gingerbread. Pre-orders closed Nov. 3, and in-store pickup begins Friday, Nov. 20. The shop also recently added a 16-piece Signature Caramel collection and, since spring, has hosted Table Tastings, small seated pairings of chocolate, artisanal cheese and non-alcoholic cocktails around its production table.

Melt Chocolates, 2018 S. 1st St., Suite 120. Factory store hours: Thursday 5-8 p.m., Friday 1-8 p.m., Saturday noon-7 p.m. More at meltchocolates.us and on Instagram at @meltchocolatesltd.
