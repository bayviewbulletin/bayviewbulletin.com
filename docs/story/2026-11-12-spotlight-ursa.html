Business spotlight · Thursday, November 12, 2026

Spotlight: URSA

URSA opened in the summer of 2017 in the historic King Building at 2534 S. Kinnickinnic Ave., right across the street from the Bay View Library. Owner Emily Kopplin hand-picks what fills the airy, high-ceilinged storefront: jewelry, apothecary and self-care goods, home décor, candles, prints, clothing and plenty of potted house plants, with many pieces from local makers. This week the shop has been posting its holiday décor, a fresh batch of ornaments and a new tote bag illustrated by a local artist, plus a closet sale of the staff's own clothes. URSA is open Monday to Friday 10 to 7, Saturday 10 to 6 and Sunday 10 to 5, and it also runs a short-stay rental, URSA Lodge.
