Spotlight: URSA

Walk past the Bay View Library on KK and it's hard to miss URSA: floor-to-ceiling windows in the historic King Building at 2534 S. Kinnickinnic Ave., with plants, pottery and handwoven bags stacked on wooden shelves inside. The shop opened in the summer of 2017 and threw a grand-opening party that October, and it has been one of the anchors of KK's boutique strip ever since.

URSA is run by Emily Sturdy (who opened it as Emily Kopplin) and her husband, John. Emily spent more than 15 years working in retail before going out on her own, and she hand-picks everything on the floor. The shop calls itself a lifestyle store, which in practice means a little of everything: jewelry, candles, incense and perfume, skin care, home décor, kitchen goods, greeting cards, prints, clothing and a healthy stock of potted houseplants. URSA has also made its own apothecary line, sold in the shop and at other stores around the Midwest, and many of its vendors are local artists and makers.

The Sturdys have a habit of helping neighbors, too. During the pandemic, URSA used Nonfiction Natural Wines' old storefront as a pickup spot for online orders, and in late 2023 the couple turned that space at 800 E. Potter Ave. into Timeshare, a rotating pop-up room for artists, vintage sellers and makers. The shop has even hosted the occasional concert.

This week URSA has been posting new arrivals, holiday décor and ornaments as the gift season ramps up. The big one for bargain hunters: an URSA Closet Sale this Sunday, Nov. 15, from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., with pre-loved clothing, shoes and accessories from the staff's own closets.

URSA, 2534 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. Open Monday to Friday 10 a.m. to 7 p.m., Saturday 10 to 6 and Sunday 10 to 5. Shop online at shopursa.com or follow @shopursa on Instagram.
