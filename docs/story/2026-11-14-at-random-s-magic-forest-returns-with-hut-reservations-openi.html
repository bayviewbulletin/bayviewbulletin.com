At Random's Magic Forest returns, with hut reservations opening Nov. 25

At Random, the cocktail lounge at 2501 S. Delaware Ave., says its Magic Forest is coming back for the holidays. The patio becomes a winter hideaway of heated cocktail huts and holiday lights, a seasonal tradition at the bar.

Reservations open Nov. 25, and the bar strongly recommends booking. Hut charges are added to your tab; check the post for current pricing. Kids are welcome until 8 p.m., and it's 21 and over after that. An opening date for the huts hasn't been posted yet.
