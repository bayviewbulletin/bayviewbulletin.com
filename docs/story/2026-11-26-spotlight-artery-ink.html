Spotlight: Artery Ink

Artery Ink makes clothing and gifts covered in detailed, anatomically accurate drawings of the human body: hearts, lungs, brains, spines, the digestive tract. The company works out of a studio at 2625 S. Greeley St. in Bay View, on the west side of the neighborhood near Russell Avenue, and sells mostly online, with local pickup at the studio.

It was started in 2014 by Mara Natkin, a trained illustrator, and Gloria Ramirez, a graphic designer, who met while waitressing and are now married. After losing relatives within a short span in 2013, the couple told WUWM, they took a hard look at their own health, got fascinated by how the body works and decided to put that into their art. They began with greeting cards and prints, sold at markets and shows, then quit their restaurant jobs to do it full time.

Every design is drawn by hand: Natkin lays out the piece, and Ramirez fills in the fine interior detail with a pen. Some of the more involved drawings take more than 80 hours, according to the shop's website. To get the anatomy right, they worked with a doctor and visited a cadaver lab. Health care workers became their biggest fans, and the catalog now runs from nurse, ICU and radiology shirts to awareness gear, city-themed "City Hearts" designs, stickers, pins and custom team orders.

Giving back is built in. In spring 2020 they released a "Heart of Healthcare" shirt as a fundraiser, sold about 900 in the first week and donated $12,000 to a campaign feeding frontline workers. Since then their Community Collection has put out new designs each month with proceeds going to causes, mostly Wisconsin groups. The business grew from a spare bedroom to a team of seven in a 4,800-square-foot studio, and Milwaukee Magazine featured the founders in 2018 and in a 2023 Women of Distinction section.

This week: in-stock items are 15% off through Dec. 1, with no code needed, and the shop has brought back its Rare Disease Awareness zebra-ribbon design, on pre-order through Dec. 14 to support a cause. New snowman tees and restocked holiday designs went up on the site last weekend.

Artery Ink, 2625 S. Greeley St., Suite 135. Online at arteryink.com (local pickup available; the studio isn't open weekends) and on Instagram at @arteryink.
