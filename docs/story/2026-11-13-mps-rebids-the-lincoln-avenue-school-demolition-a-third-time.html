MPS rebids the Lincoln Avenue School demolition a third time

Milwaukee Public Schools threw out the bids to tear down the fire-damaged Lincoln Avenue Elementary, 1817 W. Lincoln Ave., and will re-advertise the job as a sealed competitive bid that goes to the lowest responsive, responsible bidder.

Urban Milwaukee reports the switch followed an objection from West Milwaukee-based The MRD Group, which bid about $928,000; the district had chosen an Illinois firm at about $4.8 million.

The school sits west of Bay View, but the episode is part of a broader look at MPS spending: FOX6 also raised questions this week about the district's finance office.
