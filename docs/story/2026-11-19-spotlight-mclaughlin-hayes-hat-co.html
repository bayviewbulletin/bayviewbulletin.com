Spotlight: McLaughlin & Hayes Hat Co.

Inside the former Boulevard Theatre at 2250 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. is something most cities lost decades ago: a working hat shop. McLaughlin & Hayes Hat Co. sells, makes, cleans and repairs hats for men and women, from fedoras and western hats to Derby showpieces and fascinators.

John and Kate McLaughlin opened the business in 2011 as the Brass Rooster, a men's hat shop on the Avalon Theater block. In late 2014 they moved down the street into the larger former Boulevard Theatre space and added the Hen House, a women's shop, next door. The two later merged under the McLaughlin & Hayes name, with Steve Hayes joining the McLaughlins as a co-owner. Kate spent 10 seasons as the Milwaukee Rep's hatmaker and costume craft manager; John has worn and reshaped hats since he found his grandfather's fedora as a kid.

The shop says it is home to three of roughly 15 master hatters left in the country. Custom hats are built in house after a measuring and design consultation, usually in four to six weeks, on century-old equipment that came from a retiring hatter at Henry the Hatter in Detroit. The racks also carry makers like Stetson, Bailey, Dobbs and Kangol. Repair work is done on site too: cleaning and blocking felt hats, new ribbons, brim trims, leather sweatbands and full rebuilds. One thing you can't do is order online. The owners stopped web sales so every customer talks to a person, though they still take orders by phone and ship worldwide. "A hat is like a haircut you get to put on every day," John told TMJ4.

The work travels. The shop has made headwear for stage and screen, including the Tony-winning Broadway musical "War Paint," the Metropolitan Opera and Netflix's "Peaky Blinders," and one of its fedoras had a starring role in a 2020 Super Bowl ad about Vince Lombardi. John also developed a hand-inked technique that turns felt hats into painted art. For the Kentucky Derby's 150th running in 2024, one of those, a beaver-felt fedora painted with a horse race scene, was picked from worldwide entries for the Kentucky Derby Museum's permanent collection.

With cold weather settling in, this is the season for wool and fur felt, and for getting an old favorite cleaned and reblocked. Appointments are no longer required but are still recommended for custom fittings, since they book a week or two out.

McLaughlin & Hayes Hat Co., 2250 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. Open Tuesday-Saturday 11 a.m.-5 p.m. 414-210-3715, mhhats.com, @mhhatmilmke on Instagram.
