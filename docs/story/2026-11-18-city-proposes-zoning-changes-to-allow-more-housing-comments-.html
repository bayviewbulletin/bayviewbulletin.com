City proposes zoning changes to allow more housing; comments are open through Dec. 11

Milwaukee's Department of City Development released a package of proposed zoning code changes Monday meant to carry out the city's Housing Element plan. In neighborhoods like Bay View, the drafts would allow smaller lots and homes, cottage courts of three to 12 small houses around a shared yard (with zoning board approval), more townhouses, and flag lots that could let a home be built behind an existing house on a deep lot, Urban Milwaukee reported.

The proposals would also drop the formula that caps how many apartments fit on a property, letting the building's allowed size set the limit, and add design and setback rules for larger buildings next to single-family homes. They would not change any property's zoning district, and they would not broadly allow duplexes or triplexes in single-family areas.

Nothing is final: the drafts still need review and Common Council approval. The city is taking comments online through Dec. 11, and holds a drop-in session with no presentation Friday, Nov. 20, from 1 to 5:30 p.m. at the Zeidler Municipal Building, 841 N. Broadway.
