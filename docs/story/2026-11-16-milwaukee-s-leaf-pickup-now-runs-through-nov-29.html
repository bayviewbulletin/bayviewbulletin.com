Milwaukee's leaf pickup now runs through Nov. 29

The city's Department of Public Works has extended fall leaf collection through Sunday, Nov. 29. The original deadline was this past Sunday, but many trees, including plenty in Bay View, are still dropping leaves.

Rake leaves into the street with roughly a foot of space between the pile and the curb so water can still reach the drains, or set them at the curb in paper yard bags. A live map on the city's website shows where crews are working.

Neighbors told FOX6 they remember last year, when an early snow buried leaf piles and contributed to street flooding, so it's worth getting leaves out sooner rather than later.
