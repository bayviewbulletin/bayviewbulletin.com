Spotlight: Rush-Mor Records

Long before Kinnickinnic Avenue filled up with cocktail bars and boutiques, there was a record store on the corner of KK and Potter. Rush-Mor Records, at 2635 S. Kinnickinnic Ave., opened in 1971 and bills itself as Milwaukee's oldest record store, a title local press has repeated for years. It still sells new and used vinyl, CDs, DVDs, band T-shirts and imports from around the world.

The shop has been run since 1994 by Dan DuChaine and Bill Rouleau. As Milwaukee Record has told it, Rouleau was working part-time at the store when he heard the original owner was thinking about closing. He called DuChaine that fall, and the two agreed to give it a year. That trial run is now past the 30-year mark. DuChaine told Milwaukee Magazine in 2017 that the store had outlasted CD burning, downloads, file sharing and recessions, crediting loyal customers for keeping it going.

The racks lean toward modern rock, punk and hardcore, progressive and Euro-rock, rockabilly and heavy metal, and the shop will special-order what it doesn't have. Local releases get real shelf space, including a dedicated section for the Milwaukee label Triple Eye Industries. Radio Milwaukee's 2023 Record Store Day profile described a tin ceiling, string lights and a corner of band shirts, plus owners who want first-timers to feel at ease. The staff has some longevity too: CBS 58 reported that Billy Swinger has worked there for about 30 years. The shop dog, Wolfgang, also greets visitors.

Rush-Mor's reach extends beyond its four walls. It curates a music stage at the Bay View Bash street festival each September, which Milwaukee Record has called the Bash's longest-running stage, and it backs 91.7 WMSE and the MKEpunk archive of Milwaukee punk recordings. During the 2020 Safer at Home order, the store offered curbside pickup and delivery and hid 64 oversized crayons around Bay View for a free neighborhood scavenger hunt, Urban Milwaukee reported.

What's new: earlier this month the shop wrapped up a giveaway of a band-signed test pressing of My Chemical Romance's The Black Parade, marking the album's 20th anniversary. Rush-Mor also launched a refreshed website this year, and its full inventory of new arrivals, used stock and collectibles is listed on Discogs for anyone who can't make it in. Instagram is where it announces new arrivals and in-store happenings.

Rush-Mor Records, 2635 S. Kinnickinnic Ave., (414) 481-6040. Open noon to 6 pm Tuesday through Sunday; closed Mondays. rushmor.com, Instagram @rushmorrecords.
