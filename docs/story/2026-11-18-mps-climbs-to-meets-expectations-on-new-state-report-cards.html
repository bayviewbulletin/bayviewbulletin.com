MPS climbs to 'meets expectations' on new state report cards

Milwaukee Public Schools moved up from two stars, "meets few expectations," to "meets expectations" in the state report cards the Department of Public Instruction released Tuesday, WPR reported. Statewide, 95 percent of districts earned three, four or five stars.

Within MPS, 67 schools were rated as meeting, exceeding or significantly exceeding expectations, 40 as meeting few expectations and 36 as failing to meet them. Superintendent Brenda Cassellius said students are showing growth but much work remains, with a focus on the new elementary curriculum and a new high school literacy curriculum.

Each school's report card, including Bay View's MPS schools, can be looked up on DPI's report card site.
