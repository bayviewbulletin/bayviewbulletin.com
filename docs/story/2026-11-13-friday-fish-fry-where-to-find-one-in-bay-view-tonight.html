Friday fish fry: where to find one in Bay View tonight

It's Friday in Wisconsin, so here's where Bay View fries fish. Heirloom MKE (2378 S. Howell Ave.) does Fish Fry Friday from 4 to 9 p.m.: two pieces of beer-battered cod with fries, coleslaw, tartar, buttered rye and lemon. Reservations are recommended.

Centraal Grand Cafe & Tappery (2306 S. Kinnickinnic Ave.) serves a Fridays-only crispy haddock fry with coleslaw, tartar, friets and rye, starting at 11 a.m. Hue (2699 S. Kinnickinnic Ave.) puts a Vietnamese spin on it after 4 p.m., with beer-battered swai, garlic fried rice and Asian slaw.

Dom & Phil DeMarinis (1211 E. Conway St.) lists a $14 Friday beer-batter cod fry with fries, potato pancakes or pasta, plus salad and bread. Check each spot's hours before heading out, and tell us your favorite through the tip form.
