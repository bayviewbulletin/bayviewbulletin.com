New this week: MPS chief says school closures and mergers are likely in 10-year plan

Milwaukee Public Schools Superintendent Brenda Cassellius told WISN on Monday night that the district's coming 10-year facilities plan will probably close and merge some schools. She spoke after the Great Schools Advisory Committee held its last community meeting of the year.

Cassellius said there is no short list of schools targeted, and that an earlier consultant list of five North Side schools is not part of this process. The plan weighs the district's budget, over- and under-enrolled buildings and aging facilities, alongside about $355 million in planned improvements including new and modernized schools.

She will write a draft from the community feedback, take it to affected school communities and the board, and hopes to have it ready in January. Bay View families at Fernwood, Bay View Montessori, Humboldt Park, Trowbridge and Bay View High should watch for that draft.
