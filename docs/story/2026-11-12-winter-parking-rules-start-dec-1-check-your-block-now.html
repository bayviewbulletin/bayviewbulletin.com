Winter parking rules start Dec. 1: check your block now

From Dec. 1 to March 1, there's no overnight parking on through highways and bus routes, and that includes Kinnickinnic Avenue. During a declared snow emergency, alternate-side parking is required citywide from 10 p.m. to 6 a.m. Fines escalate from $50 to $100 to $150, and repeat offenders can be towed. Sign up for the city's winter parking text alerts before the first storm.
