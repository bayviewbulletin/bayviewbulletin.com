Neighbors refill the Clarence Street Cabinet after an empty-shelf plea

The Clarence Street Cabinet, a neighbor-run free food cabinet at 326 E. Clarence St., was almost completely empty during a recent restock, and its organizer asked for help on Facebook, saying demand keeps rising heading into winter. Neighbors shared the plea in the Bay View Town Hall group.

By the next night the cabinet was full again. In a follow-up post, the organizer thanked the donors who filled it and called the response heartwarming.

The cabinet runs on take what you need, give what you can. In the comments, neighbors noted that ready-to-eat items tend to go faster than dried beans and lentils, since not everyone has the time or a kitchen to cook from scratch. Follow the cabinet's Facebook page for restock updates.
