Library branches close Thursday and Friday; here's what's open around Bay View for the holiday

All Milwaukee Public Library locations, including the Bay View branch at 2566 S. Kinnickinnic Ave., will be closed Thursday and Friday for Thanksgiving. Every branch reopens Saturday, and the Bay View branch is open today until 8 p.m. for anyone stocking up on books and movies for the long weekend.

Several neighborhood spots have posted holiday plans. Sugar Maple opens at 4 p.m. Thursday. Calogero's on Bay Street pours pre-Thanksgiving cocktails tonight and is closed Thursday, and Ian's Pizza says all its locations close for the holiday.

For Thanksgiving Eve, Bay View Bowl offers $3 games and $2.50 Hamm's from 6 p.m. to midnight (and Cafe Centraal's all-night happy hour is in today's Buzz).
