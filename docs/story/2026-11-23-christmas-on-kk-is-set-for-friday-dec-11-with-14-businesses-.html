Christmas on KK is set for Friday, Dec. 11, with 14 businesses on board so far

Christmas on KK is back. The Bay View Community Center has posted the 2026 edition of the holiday stroll for Friday, Dec. 11, from 5 to 9 p.m., with participating shops and restaurants along Kinnickinnic Avenue hosting treats, kids' crafts, raffles and holiday shopping. Last week neighbors were still asking whether it would return; now there's a date and a lineup.

The center's event listing and flyer name 14 businesses so far: Chippy's, Cozy Collective, Ela Mari Boutique, Fiber Fever, Lion's Tooth, LuLu, Mac's Pet Barkery, the Magnet Factory, Nonfiction Wine, Rush-Mor Records, Sorella, Strange Scene, Tippecanoe Herbs and URSA. Plans include a craft at Chippy's in exchange for a canned-food donation, a hot cocoa bar at Ela Mari, kids' crafts at Strange Scene and a visit with Santa at Tippecanoe Herbs.

Several stops are raising help for the community center: Nonfiction Wine will sell cookie boxes made by local chefs, Sorella plans a featured dish, and URSA and Mac's are tying raffles to food donations for the center's pantry. Organizers say more activities are coming, and businesses that want to join can reach the center's board through the event page. Alderwoman Marina Dimitrijevic shared the event too.
