City-county Holiday Drive opens today, collecting food and essentials

Mayor Cavalier Johnson and County Board Chair Marcelia Nicholson-Bovell are launching the 2026 Holiday Drive Monday morning. The city and county have run the drive every year since 2007.

Organizers are asking for food, reusable tote bags, baby wipes, deodorant and other everyday essentials. This year's donations go to the Metcalfe Park People's Pantry, which Metcalfe Park Community Bridges runs.

There are nine drop-off sites. The closest to Bay View are the Zablocki Branch Library, 3501 W. Oklahoma Ave., and the City Hall rotunda, 200 E. Wells St. Other sites include East Branch Library, the County Courthouse and the Milwaukee County Zoo.
