Buttah and Biscuit shows off the space that will be its first storefront

Buttah and Biscuit, the biscuit bakery that has been selling out of the Bay View Community Center's shared kitchen, gave followers a first look inside its own brick-and-mortar space on Saturday. In a video tour, the business said the next few weeks will go to turning the empty room into its first Buttah and Biscuit storefront.

It hasn't said where the shop is or when it will open, so we'll pass that along once it does. In the meantime, its Instagram is the place to watch for updates.
