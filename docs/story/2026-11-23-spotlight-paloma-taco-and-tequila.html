Spotlight: Paloma Taco and Tequila

Paloma Taco and Tequila is the most colorful room on lower Kinnickinnic Avenue: a taqueria and tequila bar on the ground floor of the KinetiK apartment building at 2154 S. KK. Think kaleidoscope wallpaper, sunshine-yellow chairs and pendant lights hanging from a turquoise ceiling.

It's run by restaurateur Pattie Ford, who has spent her whole career in restaurants and built a following for her cocktails and infusions at Juniper 61 on North Avenue. Her first Paloma opened in July 2020 a few blocks from there, at 5419 W. North Ave. in Washington Heights. The Bay View location, her second, opened in April 2024 after an eight-month build-out with the design firm Three Sixty. At about 2,300 square feet, it's more than twice the size of the original, according to the Milwaukee Business Journal, as reported by TMJ4.

Tacos are the main event, roughly 10 kinds from carne asada to crispy tofu and tuna poke, on flour or corn tortillas, Paloma's signature crunchy pink shell, or "naked" over lettuce. There are also elote cups, loaded fries, quesadillas, the Big Paloma Torta and the Taco Pocket, a playful riff on a fast-food classic. Vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free dishes are marked on the menu, and brunch items run until 2 p.m. The bar pours more than 75 tequilas, and there's even a dog menu: a warm tortilla with banana and peanut butter.

Paloma also caters, sells gift certificates and runs a rewards program. Its Bay View spot is one of a cluster of newer restaurants and bars at KinetiK, alongside neighbors like 17th Ward.

What's new: happy hour now runs all day on Sundays and Mondays, plus weekdays from 4 to 6 p.m. Paloma says it's taking $1 off its THC drinks until they're gone, to clear its stock before a ban takes effect. A new veggie sandwich (or the same build with New York strip) is a Bay View exclusive, and a paint-and-sip night is on the calendar for Monday, Dec. 14, at 6:30 p.m.

Paloma Taco and Tequila, 2154 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. (the website lists 2156), 414-212-8978. Open Monday through Wednesday 11 a.m. to 9 p.m., Thursday 4 to 9 p.m., Friday and Saturday 11 a.m. to 10 p.m., Sunday 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. palomatacoandtequila.com, @palomabayview on Instagram.
