A dog rescue's giving tree is up at Mac's PetDepot on KK

The rescue group JRs Pups-n-stuff has put up a holiday giving tree at Mac's PetDepot Barkery on Kinnickinnic Avenue. Shoppers grab a paper ornament, buy the supply listed on the back, and leave it at checkout; the store handles the rest, according to a volunteer's post in the Bay View Town Hall group.

The rescue has a second tree at Bad Moon Saloon on Clement Avenue, just south of Bay View, where donors can drop off wishlist items or donate through QR codes on the bin.
