Babe's Ice Cream winds down its season with BOGO specials this weekend

Babe's Ice Cream, the seasonal shop at 2264 S. Kinnickinnic Ave., says this weekend is its end-of-season sale. A sign in its Saturday post promises "Freezin' for the Season" buy-one-get-one specials on its ice cream while supplies last.

Babe's didn't post a reopening date. If you want a last cone (or a few pints for the freezer) before the cold settles in, today is the day; check its Instagram for hours.
