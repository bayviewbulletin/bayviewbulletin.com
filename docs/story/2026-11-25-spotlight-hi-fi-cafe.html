Spotlight: Hi-Fi Cafe

Hi-Fi Cafe sits in the rounded corner storefront where Kinnickinnic and Potter avenues meet, under a striped awning and a multicolored "HI-FI" sign that has marked that stretch of KK for nearly three decades. It is a counter-service coffeehouse with all-day breakfast, brunch and lunch, and a reputation as one of Bay View's longest-running hangouts.

The cafe opened around 1996 under Sage Schwarm, a Milwaukee musician who played in Wild Kingdom and Citizen King. In 2003 he sold it to Mary Hart and her partner, Peter Frederick Steinhoff, who have run it since. Milwaukee Record's "Mandatory Milwaukee" series profiled it in 2019, and Urban Milwaukee's dining column called it a time warp in 2020.

That label comes from the room itself: Formica and butcher-block tables, red vinyl chairs, neon signs, a vintage magazine rack and a jukebox, plus walls hung with work by local artists. The menu leans vegetarian-friendly and a little playful. Sandwiches carry names like Stringy Iggy (house corned beef), Jean Jeanie (hummus and veggies) and Falafel Yum Yum, and the espresso list includes a Raspberry Beret and a peanut-butter Buttercup. The coffee is Valentine's organic, fair-trade roast, and the quiche and soup change daily.

The past few years have tested the business. A September 2020 GoFundMe raised nearly 2,000, and in spring 2023 the owners launched a second campaign, with a 5,000 goal, to pay for renovations while the cafe was temporarily closed. Hart told Urban Milwaukee it was "the only place like it in Milwaukee, the Midwest" and beyond. Singer Abby Jeanne, a former Hi-Fi employee, also started a small label called Hi-Fi Records, whose first release was a holiday single recorded in the cafe's kitchen.

These days the cafe's Instagram lists it as open Wednesday through Sunday, and it stayed open until 9 p.m. for Bay View Gallery Night in September. It sells e-gift cards through its website. We couldn't find a Thanksgiving weekend schedule, so check its Instagram before heading over.

Hi-Fi Cafe, 2640 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. Open Wednesday through Sunday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. Phone 414-486-0504. Website: 5ea646d4d1216.site123.me. Instagram: @hificafemke.
