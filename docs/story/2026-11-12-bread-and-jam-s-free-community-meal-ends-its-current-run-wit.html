Bread and Jam's free community meal ends its current run with a Thanksgiving feast

Bay View United Methodist Church (2772 S. Kinnickinnic Ave.) will serve its last free community meal in its current form on Sunday, Nov. 29, from 2:30 to 3:30 p.m., with a traditional Thanksgiving feast. The church's Bread and Jam weekly meal has fed neighbors since 1994, with food from Feeding America and volunteer cooks and servers from across Bay View and the South Side. Questions go to Pastor Anna-Lisa Hunter at the church.
