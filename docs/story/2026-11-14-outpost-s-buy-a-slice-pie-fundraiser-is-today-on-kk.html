Outpost's Buy a Slice pie fundraiser is today on KK

Outpost Natural Foods is holding its annual Buy a Slice fundraiser today from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. Stop in and get a slice of the co-op's pie for a suggested $5 donation; the co-op's posts feature its apple pie.

The event runs at Outpost's stores, including the Bay View store at 2826 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. It's also a good moment to look at Thanksgiving: Outpost is taking online orders for full holiday meals, sides and desserts, and has been introducing the Wisconsin farms that raise its turkeys.
