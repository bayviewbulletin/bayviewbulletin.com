Nationwide ground stop strands travelers at Mitchell Airport

A rare nationwide ground stop held every departing flight in the U.S. on the ground for more than four hours Friday afternoon and evening, leaving travelers at Milwaukee Mitchell International Airport stuck at gates and rebooking on the fly. The FAA ordered the stop shortly before 3 p.m. Central after a telecommunications problem disrupted communications with regional air traffic control centers, according to CBS 58.

Planes already in the air were allowed to land, but departures were held until the FAA lifted the stop Friday evening. Federal officials said there was no sign of a cyberattack. It was the first nationwide grounding since an FAA computer outage in January 2023.

If you're flying out of Mitchell this weekend, check with your airline before heading to the airport: carriers warned that planes and crews left out of position could mean more delays and cancellations through the weekend. Neighbors in the Bay View Town Hall group noticed the upside, too: an unusually quiet night under the flight path.
