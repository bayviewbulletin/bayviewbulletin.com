Spotlight: Jasher's Organic Tea House

Walk through Joyce Skylight Court at 2680 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. all the way to the back, and you'll find Jasher's Organic Tea House in Suite 4. It feels more like a living room than a cafe: couches, quiet music, a shelf of board games and, out back, a pet-friendly patio.

Married couple Jack and Rumi DeDecker opened it quietly in late November 2025 and named it for their young son. The two learned the ropes selling at local farmers markets before taking on a storefront, and they still spend plenty of evenings there as a family.

The menu has more than 20 teas, and everything on it is USDA certified organic. The couple also skip flavorings of any kind, natural or artificial. Look for herbal infusions like vanilla rooibos and turmeric ginger tonic, green and white teas, ceremonial matcha, yerba mate and pu'er tuo cha, a compressed Chinese tea that holds up to several steepings. The best seller is Rumi's masala chai, made fresh daily with black Assam tea, fresh ginger, whole spices and jaggery. Jack told Urban Milwaukee, "I would say it's a top seller." Any tea can be iced, the butterfly-pea Blue & Violet Tango changes color when you squeeze in lemon, and this fall there's organic apple cider from Peck & Bushel in Colgate, spiced if you like. Snacks include popcorn popped in coconut and olive oil and scones and cakes from Rise & Shine Bakery.

Jasher's threw a three-day grand opening in June 2026, with a loyalty card launch, a tea-tasting contest and live music. It also finished a two-phase restoration of its patio, and in July it took its masala chai and iced teas to the Incredible India Festival at the Summerfest grounds. Lately it has hosted a Saturday morning run club and a community kit-packing event.

This week brings something new. On Friday, Nov. 20, Milwaukee Symphony Orchestra violinist Paul Hauer plays a solo set from 7 to 7:45 p.m., followed by an acoustic worship set led by Jack until 8:30. There will be free samples of a new chocolate mint chai, a blend of organic pu'er, cocoa shells, cinnamon, peppermint, vanilla bean and licorice root.

Jasher's Organic Tea House, 2680 S. Kinnickinnic Ave., Suite 4 (inside Joyce Skylight Court). Open Tuesday-Friday 4-9 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m.-5 p.m.; closed Sunday and Monday. jashersorganic.com, @jashersorganicteahouse on Instagram.
