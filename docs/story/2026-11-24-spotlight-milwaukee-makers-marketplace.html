Spotlight: Milwaukee Makers Marketplace

Milwaukee Makers Marketplace is a store with one simple rule: the people who stock its shelves made what they sell. It sits in Suite 135 of the Hide House, the former tannery complex at 2625 S. Greeley St. that now holds studios, shops and small businesses, and it carries the work of dozens of local artists and makers: wall art, ceramics, jewelry, candles, apparel and more.

The shop grew out of the Milwaukee Makers Market, the pop-up market run by owner Ryan Laessig. It started in 2017 as a small outdoor market in the Colectivo parking lot at Kinnickinnic and Lincoln avenues, a few blocks away, and grew into a monthly market at venues like Discovery World, American Family Field and The Ivy House. Shepherd Express readers have named it the city's best art and craft fair or makers market five times, most recently in 2025, according to the market's website. In September 2024 Laessig opened the permanent store, telling Radio Milwaukee that spending there goes "right back into the local maker economy." (That Radio Milwaukee piece was written with information from the shop, a sponsor of the station.)

Walk in and you'll find bracelets from Linny's Artsy Fingers, upcycled vintage jewelry from SmitTin, handmade goods from Makery Maven Co. and clothing from Slacker Posse and Giltee Apparel, along with longtime market vendors like Big White Yeti, Nourish Natural Products, Maebel Jewelry and Bay View's own Artery Ink, which is also in the Hide House. A corner of the store doubles as a gallery with rotating exhibitions by local artists. One Bay View-flavored find, according to OnMilwaukee's new food-inspired gift guide: a six-panel cotton cap inspired by the South Shore Farmers Market, made with help from Bay View Threadworks, for $40.

This month has been busy. The gallery opened "Happy Accidents," a first show by an artist named Alexis, on Nov. 14, and the shop thanked Shepherd Express for including it in this year's holiday gift guide. Extended holiday hours run through Dec. 31, though the store is closed on Thanksgiving.

The market side is gearing up, too. Milwaukee Makers Market hosts what it calls Milwaukee's official Shop Small Saturday at Discovery World this Saturday, Nov. 28, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., with more than 50 local small businesses, food trucks and free admission. A holiday market follows there on Sunday, Dec. 20.

Milwaukee Makers Marketplace, 2625 S. Greeley St., Suite 135 (inside Hide House). Holiday hours: Thursday 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., Friday and Saturday 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., Sunday 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. (closed Thanksgiving). milwaukeemakersmarket.com; Instagram @milwaukeemakersmarket.
