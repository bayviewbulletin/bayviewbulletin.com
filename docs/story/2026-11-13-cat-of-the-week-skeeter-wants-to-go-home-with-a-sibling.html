Cat of the Week: Skeeter wants to go home with a sibling

Milwaukee Record and Urban Cat Coalition's Cat of the Week is Skeeter, an orange tabby born in April. He's neutered, vaccinated and house-trained, and gets along with kids and other cats.

The catch: he has to be adopted with at least one of his siblings, Zinnia, Mango or Frodo. Details and the adoption application are at the link.
