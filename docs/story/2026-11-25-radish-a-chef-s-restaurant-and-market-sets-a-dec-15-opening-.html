Radish, a chef's restaurant and market, sets a Dec. 15 opening on Delaware Avenue

Radish, the restaurant and small market from Bay View-raised chef Micaela Erickson, now has an opening date. The business announced on Instagram that it will open Tuesday, Dec. 15, with a special holiday menu, and it shared a first look at a bright dining room with globe lights, a long counter and bentwood stools.

Radish's website lists its home as 2591 S. Delaware Ave., at the corner of Delaware and Pryor avenues. That's a change from the plan Erickson announced in January 2025, when the project was headed for a tiny storefront at 2986 S. Kinnickinnic Ave. The site describes a seasonal menu, a thoughtfully sourced market and low-intervention wines.

Planned hours on the site are 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. Tuesday and Wednesday and 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. Thursday through Saturday, closed Sunday and Monday, with no reservations. Erickson ran a private-chef business, also called Radish, for years and previously cooked at La Merenda, Bavette La Boucherie and Ca'Lucchenzo, according to Urban Milwaukee's 2025 report on the project.
