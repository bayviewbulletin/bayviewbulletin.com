Police oversight board holds off on recommending an end to Milwaukee's license plate cameras

Milwaukee's Fire and Police Commission on Thursday night delayed a vote on a resolution urging the Police Department to stop using automatic license plate readers, including the Flock cameras mounted on poles around the city, WISN reported.

Commission Vice Chair Krissie Fung, who brought the proposal, questioned whether the cameras' crime-solving benefits outweigh privacy risks, while Commissioner Ruben Burgos argued the technology works and the city could switch vendors instead. Commissioners pressed police on what data is stored and who can see it, including outside companies and federal agencies, and dozens of residents spoke in favor of a ban.

The vote to hold the resolution means it could return in amended form, but no date has been set. Police told WISN in October that they planned to renew the Flock contract soon.
