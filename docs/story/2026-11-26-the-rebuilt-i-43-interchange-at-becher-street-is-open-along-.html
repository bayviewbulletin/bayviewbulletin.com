The rebuilt I-43 interchange at Becher Street is open, along with 4th and 5th streets

The I-43/94 interchange at Becher Street, one of the main freeway connections on Bay View's west edge, reopened to traffic Wednesday, the state Department of Transportation said. S. 4th and 5th streets, the frontage streets alongside the freeway, reopened too.

The whole interchange, including Becher Street under the freeway, had been closed for reconstruction since spring, with detours sending drivers to Lapham Boulevard, 1st Street and 6th Street. The old scissor-style ramps are gone, replaced by a standard diamond interchange with rebuilt traffic signals at both ramp ends, one through lane each way on Becher under the freeway, and updated curb ramps.

WisDOT's project page has the new ramp layout and a project contact for questions.
