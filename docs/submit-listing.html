Submit your Bay View listing

Listing a home, condo, duplex or commercial space in Bay View? Send it to us and we’ll feature it on our Real Estate page, on the homepage and in our Saturday “On the market” post on Facebook and Instagram. It’s free.

Include: the address, price, beds and baths, square footage, open house dates and times, and a link to the listing. To include photos, attach them and choose the “Yes” photo option on the form: that confirms you own the photos or have the photographer’s permission, and lets us use them with credit to you and your brokerage.

We describe each listing in our own words, link to your listing page, and credit your brokerage. We check every submission against the listing before it runs, and we don’t run listings outside Bay View (the harbor south to Holt and Morgan, I-43 east to the lake).
