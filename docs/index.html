Spotlight: Ormson Supply Co.

Mid-century furniture, records and a taxidermied deer in the window, on KK since 2020

Ormson Supply Co. is a vintage shop at 2866 S. Kinnickinnic Ave., on the corner storefront with the big plate-glass windows (and, often, a taxidermied deer standing guard). It sells mid-century modern furniture, much of it Scandinavian, along with records, clothing, housewares and whatever else the owners think is worth hauling home.
